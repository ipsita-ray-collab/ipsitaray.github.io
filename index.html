About

A broad research program in fundamental physics

My work has focused on heavy-flavor phenomenology, particularly semileptonic and rare B-meson decays within effective field theory. My current and emerging interests extend to non-leptonic B decays, dark matter effective field theory, neutrino physics, quantum information science, astrophysical data analysis, and machine learning.

Research

Flavor & effective field theory

Studies of B-meson decays, flavor symmetries, CP violation, Wilson coefficients, hadronic effects, and tests of physics beyond the Standard Model.

Emerging directions

Quantum & computational physics

Quantum tomography, open quantum systems, dark matter and neutrino physics, machine learning, data science, and connections between particle physics and astrophysics.

Academic profile

Researcher and educator
