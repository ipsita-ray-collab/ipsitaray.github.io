Teaching & mentoring

Teaching

I aim to create an active, inclusive learning environment in which students build conceptual understanding, critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and computational abilities.

Teaching philosophy

Learning through understanding and participation

My teaching philosophy emphasizes active learning, peer interaction, hands-on training, independent problem solving, and the integration of computational methods into theoretical physics. I favor boardwork for developing intermediate steps and use questions, group activities, and reasoning-focused assessment to keep students actively involved.

Conceptual understanding

Mathematical formalism is paired with physical intuition and opportunities for students to solve problems independently.

Computational physics

Students are encouraged to develop numerical and technical skills alongside analytical methods.

Collaborative learning

Group assignments and peer interaction help students communicate ideas and learn from different perspectives.

Inclusive community

Outreach, diverse speakers, and interdisciplinary connections can help students see themselves as participants in science.
