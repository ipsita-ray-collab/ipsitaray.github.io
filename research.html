Research

My research spans particle physics phenomenology, with a core focus on heavy-flavor physics and effective field theory, together with growing interests in quantum information, dark matter, neutrinos, astrophysics, and machine learning.

Past research

Flavor physics and effective field theory

b → sℓℓ transitions

Data-driven studies of new-physics effects in rare B and Bs decays using dimension-6 effective operators, including model selection with AICc and cross-validation.

b → dℓℓ transitions

Studies of B → πℓℓ and B → ρℓℓ modes, including direct CP-asymmetric observables and their sensitivity to different new-physics scenarios.

Determination of |Vub| and |Vcb|

Reanalyses of exclusive semileptonic B decays and combined studies of b → uℓν and b → cℓν channels to investigate CKM determinations.

B → D(*)ℓν and new physics

Studies of possible new-physics scenarios in B̄ → D(*)ℓν decays, including scenarios with different effects in heavy- and light-lepton modes.

B-meson distribution amplitudes

Constraints on inverse moments of B and Bs meson light-cone distribution amplitudes using lattice-QCD inputs and form-factor analyses.

Non-local effects in Bs → φℓℓ

Analysis of higher-twist three-particle Bs-meson LCDAs and their impact on non-local form factors and charm-loop contributions.

B → πK and isospin

Application of isospin-based electroweak-penguin/tree relations to the B → πK amplitudes and their implications for the observed branching ratios and CP asymmetries.

Current projects

Expanding toward new frontiers

B → VV decays

Study of B-meson decays to two vector mesons using SU(3)F × SU(2)spin symmetry and related hadronic observables.

Dark Matter Effective Field Theory

Exploration of dark-sector scenarios and their interplay with flavor physics and experimental constraints.

Quantum information in particle physics

Development of quantum-tomography approaches for hadronic systems and exploration of quantum-information concepts in high-energy physics.

Neutrinos and open quantum systems

Future work connecting neutrino physics, gravitational interactions, oscillations, dense neutrino systems, and possible implications for cosmological asymmetries.

Particle physics, cosmology & ML

Machine-learning approaches to BSM scenarios, electroweak phase transitions, dark matter, axion-like particles, and cosmological observables.

Astrophysics & multi-messenger science

Interests include gamma-ray excesses, dark matter models, dark energy, galaxy simulations, transient astronomy, gravitational waves, and machine-learning methods for astrophysical surveys.
