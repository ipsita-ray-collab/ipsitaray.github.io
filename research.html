Research program

Broad research interests

◆

Flavor Physics and Effective Field Theory: Neutral Current Transitions

Studies of rare flavor-changing neutral-current decays, with particular emphasis on b → sℓℓ and b → dℓℓ transitions. These analyses use effective field theory, statistical model selection, CP-sensitive observables, and hadronic inputs to investigate possible physics beyond the Standard Model.

b → sℓℓ analysis · Nucl. Phys. B 969 (2021) 115479 ↗ b → dℓℓ analysis · JHEP 03 (2023) 247 ↗
◆

Flavor Physics: Charged Current Transitions and New Physics

The semi-leptonic charged current transitions provide precision probes of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa (CKM) matrix elements and possible new interactions.

A closer look at the extraction of |Vub| from B → πℓν ↗ Extractions of |Vub|/|Vcb| from a combined study of the exclusive b → u(c)ℓνℓ decays · JHEP 07 (2023) 024 ↗ Test of new physics effects in B̄ → (D(*), π)ℓ−ν̄ℓ decays with heavy and light leptons ↗
◆

Light Cone Sum Rules

Constraining the inverse moment of the B-meson distribution amplitudes using Lattice QCD inputs, and analysing impact of higher-twist three particle Light Cone Distribution Amplitudes on the non-local form factors.

Constraining inverse moment of B-meson distribution amplitude using Lattice QCD data ↗ Probing the inverse moment of Bs-meson distribution amplitude via Bs → ηs form factors ↗ Investigating non-local contributions in Bs → φℓℓ including higher-twist effects ↗
◆

Flavor physics: Non-leptonic decays

I have also studied B → πK amplitudes using isospin-based electroweak-penguin/tree relations, with an interest in flavor symmetries for non-leptonic B decays.

Isospin-based EWP-tree Relations ↗ Anomalies in Hadronic B → VV Decays ↗
◆

Dark Matter Effective Field Theory

Exploring the interplay between low-energy flavor physics and dark matter phenomenology within dimension-six dark-sector effective field theories, including constraints from relic density, direct and indirect detection, and flavor observables.

Benchmark Dark SMEFT Scenarios · arXiv:2609.26886 ↗
◆

Quantum information in particle physics, neutrinos and open quantum systems

Applying quantum-information concepts such as quantum state tomography, entanglement, and open quantum systems to particle-physics and hadronic systems, alongside interests in neutrino oscillations, neutrino magnetic moments, and dense neutrino environments.

Current projects include quantum tomography of hadronic systems and studies of neutrino magnetic moments; manuscripts are in preparation.

◆

Particle physics, cosmology & machine learning

Exploring connections between particle physics and cosmology, including dark matter, baryogenesis, first-order electroweak phase transitions, axion-like particles, astrophysical observations, and machine-learning methods for physics and cosmological data analysis.
